MECHANICS: APPLY ONLY RELEVANT RULES
- Establish the isolated body/section and equilibrium before stress calculations. Distinguish axial N, shear V, bending M and torsional T. Use consistent axes and perpendicular moment arms. For CCW-positive moments, signs must follow Mz=rx*Fy-ry*Fx. A negative result reverses an assumed arrow.
- For distributed loads, use the resultant/centroid on the selected segment and recalculate intensity at a cut. A triangular resultant acts one-third of the base from the larger-intensity end. Use direct geometry when sufficient; no unused integrals.
- Use actual solid, annular, net or built-up resisting area without double-counting overlaps. Average direct shear V/A is not maximum beam shear stress. Determine actual shear planes and force per fastener; equal sharing needs justification. Check specified failure modes and select the governing allowable load. Do not apply safety factors twice to allowable stresses.
- Use tension-positive axial force/strain and compression-negative unless another convention is shown. Preserve signed displacement/direction. Strain uses ORIGINAL length: epsilon=(Lprime-L0)/L0. Engineering shear strain is the signed change from a right angle in radians; distinguish gamma from gamma/2.
- Combine equilibrium with compatibility for rigid bars/indeterminate systems. A rigid bar's small-rotation displacement field is linear; equal endpoint displacement does not imply equal reactions. Include moving supports and actual stiffness AE/L. Wires carry tension only. Sum signed NL/(AE); integrate only for continuous variation. Use exact deformed geometry when needed and identify small-angle approximations mathematically.
- Use sigma=E*epsilon and tau=G*gamma only in their linear-elastic range. Obtain E from the initial straight-line graph slope. Select the correct tension/compression curve and loading segment; show interpolation. Do not extrapolate the initial elastic line through plastic loading.
- Follow the stated unloading law. For linear elastic unloading with slope E: recovered strain=sigma_max/E; permanent strain=epsilon_total-sigma_max/E; permanent extension=permanent strain times original length. Distinguish total, recovered and permanent deformation.
- For isotropic linear elasticity, epsilon_lateral=-nu*epsilon_axial and G=E/[2(1+nu)]. Diameter change=epsilon_lateral*d0; add it to d0 only when final diameter is requested. Include gaps/thermal effects only when the problem requires them.
- Keep units consistent: 1 MPa=1 N/mm^2; 1 GPa=1000 N/mm^2; 1 kN=1000 N; 1 kip=1000 lbf; 1 ksi=1000 psi; 1 ft=12 in. Strain is dimensionless; angular strain/rotation uses radians unless another unit is requested.
- For topics beyond the examples use the appropriate standard mechanics model and necessary assumptions; never claim a nonexistent source table/example.
